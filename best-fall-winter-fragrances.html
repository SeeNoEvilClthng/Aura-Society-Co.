Best Fall & Winter Fragrances

Warm tobacco, creamy vanilla, glowing amber and spice: the scents that feel like a cashmere sweater.

What makes a great cold-weather scent?

Cold air dulls fresh, citrusy fragrances, but it makes warm notes shine. In fall and winter, reach for tobacco, vanilla, amber, tonka bean, cinnamon, leather and woods. These richer notes last longer on skin and wool, and they create that cozy trail people remember.

Our quick picks

  • Best overall: Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille. Pipe tobacco, vanilla and spice: the definitive winter scent.
  • Best for date night: Kilian Angels' Share. Cognac, cinnamon and praline that smells like dessert by a fireplace.
  • Most cozy: Maison Margiela Replica By the Fireplace. Roasted chestnuts, smoke and vanilla.
  • Best value: Lattafa Khamrah. Dates, cinnamon, praline and vanilla with huge longevity for $59.
  • Best for her: YSL Black Opium. Coffee and vanilla with a sweet floral heart.
  • Best for the office: Parfums de Marly Herod. Warm tobacco and vanilla that stays refined, never loud.

How to make warm scents last

  • Moisturize first: fragrance lasts longer on hydrated skin.
  • Spray on your scarf or sweater, where warm notes linger for days.
  • Go lighter indoors: two sprays of a rich scent go a long way in heated rooms.
