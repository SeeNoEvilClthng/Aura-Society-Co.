Best Baccarat Rouge 540 Alternatives

The famous saffron-amber glow, from $59. Here's how the best alternatives compare to the original, and which one to pick.

What makes Baccarat Rouge 540 so popular?

Maison Francis Kurkdjian's Baccarat Rouge 540 is one of the most recognizable scents in the world. It opens with saffron and jasmine, then settles into a glowing blend of amberwood, ambergris and fir resin. The result is airy, sweet and almost sugary, with a trail people notice from across the room.

The catch is the price: the original sells for around $350 for 70mL at department stores. That's why a whole family of fragrances now follows the same saffron-amberwood style for a fraction of the cost.

The Original

Still the smoothest and most refined, if you want the real thing.

The Best Alternatives

Same saffron-amber style, each with its own personality.

How they compare

  • Lattafa Ana Abiyedh Rouge: the closest match on a budget. Bright saffron and sweet amber on the first spray, with good longevity for the price.
  • Orientica Amber Rouge: the richest and deepest of the group. Warmer and a little sweeter than the original, and it lasts all day. It also comes in a beautiful jeweled bottle, which makes it a great gift.
  • Dossier Ambery Saffron: the lightest and most airy. Closest to the original's transparent feel, and perfect if you don't like heavy scents.
  • Al Haramain Amber Oud Rouge: the boldest. A louder, stronger take with extra projection for nights out.

Should you get the original or an alternative?

If Baccarat Rouge 540 is your signature scent, the original is worth it: its drydown is smoother and more refined. If you want the vibe for everyday wear, want to layer, or are buying a gift on a budget, an alternative gets you very close for about a fifth of the price. Many people own both: the original for special occasions and an alternative for daily use.

Tips for wearing a saffron-amber scent

  • Two or three sprays is plenty; these scents project strongly.
  • Spray on skin and clothes: amber notes cling to fabric and last even longer there.
  • They work year-round, but shine brightest in fall and winter.
