Best Creed Aventus Alternatives

The smoky pineapple king, from $59. How the best alternatives stack up against the original.

Why everyone wants Aventus

Creed Aventus is the best-selling men's niche fragrance of the last decade. It opens with juicy pineapple, black currant, bergamot and apple, then dries down into smoky birch, musk and oakmoss. Fruity, smoky and fresh at once, it works at the office, on a date and everywhere in between.

At around $495 for 100mL at retail, it's also one of the most expensive. That made it the most "inspired-by" fragrance in the world, and a few of those alternatives have become cult favorites in their own right.

The Original

Smooth, balanced and refined, at half of retail.

The Best Alternatives

Smoky, fruity and fresh, each with its own twist.

How they compare

  • Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man: the legend. A sharp, smoky lemon and pineapple opening that settles into birch and musk. Huge projection and longevity. It's a bit harsher in the first 10 minutes, then gets very close.
  • Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Overdose: a richer, rounder take on Club de Nuit Intense with more depth and a smoother opening.
  • Afnan Supremacy Silver: smoother and fruitier from the first spray, with less smoke. The easiest one to wear for beginners.
  • Al Haramain Amber Oud Carbon Edition: the freshest and most modern of the group, with a clean smoky-fruity balance.
  • Lattafa Qaed Al Fursan: the pineapple lover's pick. Juicy, bright pineapple up front with soft woods. Great for summer.

Original or alternative?

Aventus is still the most balanced and refined; its smoky drydown is smooth in a way the alternatives don't fully match. But Club de Nuit Intense and Supremacy Silver get remarkably close for about an eighth of the price, and they're great for everyday wear, for the gym bag, or as a gift. Plenty of fragrance fans keep both.

When to wear it

  • Spring and summer days, especially outdoors.
  • Office and daytime: two sprays is enough.
  • Date nights: the fruity-smoky combo gets compliments.
